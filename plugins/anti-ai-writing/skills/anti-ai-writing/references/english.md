# English text

Read this only when the text is in English. The principles, modes, voice profile, never-inject list and checklist from SKILL.md apply unchanged; the Russian language rules (§6) do not. For deep English editing, [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) is the reference catalog.

## Dashes and quotes

- No em dash. In casual channels (a post typed in a web form, a quick email) people type a hyphen or no dash at all. In edited documents use commas, colons or parentheses; an en dash is acceptable for ranges (2024–2026).
- Straight quotes in casual channels; curly quotes only where the editor or target format sets them. Do not mix.

## Strongest tells (act on one sighting)

- Empty contrast: "It's not just X, it's Y", "This isn't about X. It's about Y", "X rather than Y" as a punchline, a clipped tail ", no guessing".
- One-line closers: "That's the real win.", "Let that sink in.", "Read that again.", a sentence after an example that explains what it showed.
- Staged run-ups: "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing", "Honestly?", "Real talk", "Here's what you need to know".
- Arguing with no one: "I'm not saying…", "To be clear…", "A tempting approach would be…".
- Chatbot residue: "Great question!", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if you'd like…", "Certainly!".
- Self-labelled candor: "I'll be direct", "the honest answer is", "no fluff".

## Words and phrases

Current layer (GPT-5 era): emphasizing, highlighting, showcasing, enhance, underscore (verb), crucial, vital role, stands as a testament, rich heritage, enduring legacy, broader trends, quietly, in short (Claude Opus 5.5 uses it 11 times more than Opus 5).

Legacy layer (2023–2025), still reads as AI: delve, tapestry, realm, intricate, pivotal, meticulous, multifaceted, foster, leverage (verb), utilize, robust (figurative), seamless, landscape (figurative), embark, harness, empower, navigate (figurative), unlock, elevate, groundbreaking, transformative, game-changer, cutting-edge, holistic.

N-grams (more reliable than single words): provide valuable insights, unwavering commitment, open new avenues, complex and multifaceted, intricate interplay, play a significant role in shaping, it's worth noting, at its core.

Replacements: furthermore/moreover/additionally → also, and, plus. utilize/leverage → use. comprehensive → full. robust → solid. crucial/pivotal → key. serves as, boasts, features → is, has.

## Language habits

- Contractions: don't, it's, that's.
- Start sentences with And, But, So when natural. Fragments are fine in moderation.
- Repeat a word instead of cycling synonyms ("the tool", "the platform", "the solution").
- Sentence case headings, not Title Case.
- Hyphenate compound modifiers before a noun (a high-quality report), not after it (the report is high quality).
- Writing in a second language often reads "sterile" and triggers suspicion. The cure is the same: concrete details, personal facts, varied syntax.

## Channels

- LinkedIn: no broetry (one-line paragraphs separated by blank lines), no "I'm humbled/excited to announce", no "Agree?" bait, no emoji bullets.
- Email: do not open with "I hope this email finds you well". First line is context or the point.
